EG-02 / Capabilities

Everything runs on-device.

🌐

Deep Packet Inspection

A local VPN inspects every packet. Detects cleartext HTTP, PII exfiltration, and suspicious destinations — without sending traffic anywhere.

🎣

Phishing Detection

Scans SMS, notifications, and URLs with on-device ML. Malicious links are flagged before you tap them. Content never leaves your phone.

👀

Stalkerware Scanning

Analyzes installed apps and permission patterns to flag spyware, stalkerware, and apps with excessive access to camera, microphone, or location.

🤖

Private AI Companion

Ask security questions and analyse suspicious content with a local GGUF model. Fully offline by default — cloud mode is opt-in only with your own API key.

🔐

Encrypted Storage

All scan data, signatures, and settings are encrypted with AES-256-GCM via Android Keystore. Hardware-backed keys with periodic rotation.

📱

IPv6 Ready

Full IPv6 packet parsing with extension header support. Fail-closed design means no uninspected traffic gets through.

EG-07 / FAQ

Questions & answers.

Does Embergard send any of my data to the cloud?

No. All scanning, AI inference, and storage happens on your Android device. The only network requests are for signature/model updates and subscription validation via Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. Embergard does not require an email, phone number, or account. Subscriptions are handled through Google Play on your device.

What permissions does Embergard need?

VPN permission for traffic inspection, SMS/notification access for phishing detection, and storage for local malware scanning. Every permission is requested just-in-time and explained.

Is the AI really offline?

Yes, by default. Models are downloaded as GGUF files and run through the local Rust engine. Cloud mode is opt-in only, using an API key you provide.

Who makes Embergard?

Embergard is a product brand of Aethel Guard, based in Amsterdam, The Netherlands.
