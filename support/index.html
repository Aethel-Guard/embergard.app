Support.

Common questions

  • Is my data sent to the cloud? No. All scanning, analysis, and chat happen on your device.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Cancel through Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • What does the Personal subscription include? Unlimited scans, unlimited AI chat, continuous auto-scanning, AI threat detection, deepfake detection, emergency key rotation, and Selene Shield — for one device.
  • Why does the app request VPN permission? The VPN is an on-device loopback used for deep-packet inspection and traffic analysis. Your traffic is not routed to external servers.
  • How do I delete my data? Uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings. See Delete Your Data.
